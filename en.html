03 / My approach

Listen. Plan.
Adapt, together.

An individual training programme takes shape through conversation, practice and regular progress reviews.

  1. 01/

    We get to know each other

    A one-hour initial meeting, in person or online, to talk about you, your habits and what you’re looking for from training.

  2. 02/

    We set a direction

    We clarify your goals and starting point, taking into account the time, space and equipment available to you.

  3. 03/

    We work on your training

    In person, I guide you through your training. Online, you follow a personalised programme with the support included in your chosen option.

  4. 04/

    We review your progress

    We discuss how training is going and what needs adjusting. With online coaching, updates follow your monthly or weekly check-in plan.

04 / Frequently asked questions

Before you
get started.

Practical information for our first meeting.

Another question? Get in touch
How do I request a first appointment?+

You can message me on WhatsApp, send an email or call me. Let me know whether you prefer to meet in person or online: we’ll agree on the place, date and time together. Sending a message is a request, not a confirmed booking.

How long is a session and where does it take place?+

The first meeting lasts one hour and can take place in person or online. I work at Canottieri Olona 1894 in Milan and Milago Fitness in Buccinasco. Personal training sessions also last one hour.

Do I need to be fit already?+

No. These options are for beginners as well as people who already train. Your starting point and personal goals guide the work we do together.

How does online coaching work?+

Monthly coaching includes a personalised 4-week programme, updated every month. The weekly check-in option includes feedback each week and ongoing updates. For a specific or performance goal, duration and cost depend on the goal and the time available before the event.

What should I wear and bring?+

Clothing and equipment requirements will depend on the type of first meeting. Ask me when you get in touch, and let me know whether we’ll meet in person or online.

Can I request physiotherapy treatment?+

I am currently a Physiotherapy student, not a physiotherapist. The services I offer here are personal training and coaching; they do not include physiotherapy or rehabilitation treatments.

How do I pay for coaching packages?+

Coaching packages require payment in advance. The level of support depends on your chosen option. Contact me directly to agree on the payment method.
